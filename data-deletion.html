Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 15 November 2026

We respect your right to control your personal data. If you would like us to delete the data we have collected through your interactions with our services on Meta platforms (Facebook Messenger, Instagram), please follow the instructions below.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send an email to eslam.s.m153@gmail.com with the subject line: "Data Deletion Request".
  2. In the email, include:
    1. Your full name as it appears on your Meta (Facebook/Instagram) profile.
    2. The Meta platform you used to contact us (Facebook Messenger and/or Instagram).
    3. Any additional details to help us identify your conversation (e.g., approximate date of contact).
  3. We will confirm receipt of your request within 2 business days.
  4. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of the confirmed request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon a valid deletion request, we will delete:

  • All messages and conversation history stored in our customer-support platform.
  • Your profile information (name, profile picture) as received from Meta.
  • Your Meta-scoped user ID associated with our page/account.
  • Any contact details you shared during conversations.

What We Cannot Delete

We cannot delete:

  • Data stored by Meta on their platforms — please refer to Meta's data-deletion options.
  • Data we are legally required to retain (e.g., for tax or regulatory compliance).

Alternative: Remove App Access via Facebook

You can also remove our app's access to your Facebook data directly:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Find Abu-elkhir CRM in the list.
  3. Click Remove.

This revokes our app's access but does not delete data already stored. To fully delete stored data, please also send the email request described above.
