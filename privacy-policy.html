Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Eslam Senosy Mahmoud ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our services through the Abu-elkhir CRM application and related tools integrated with Meta platforms (Facebook Messenger, Instagram).

1. Information We Collect

When you send us a message through Facebook Messenger or Instagram Direct, we receive:

  • Your public profile name and profile picture as provided by Meta.
  • The content of messages you send us (text, images, attachments).
  • Your Meta-scoped user ID (an identifier specific to our page/account).
  • Timestamps of your messages.

We do not collect any data beyond what you voluntarily send us or what Meta provides as part of the messaging interaction.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Customer support: To respond to your inquiries and provide the services you request.
  • Appointment booking: To schedule and manage appointments at Abu El Khair clinic.
  • Service improvement: To understand common questions and improve our response quality.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your messages are stored on our self-hosted customer-support platform (Chatwoot) on a secured server. We apply industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS), access controls, and regular updates. Only authorized team members can access conversations.

4. Data Retention

We retain conversation data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 7).

5. Third-Party Services

Our application integrates with Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram) to receive and respond to messages. Your use of Facebook Messenger or Instagram is also subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at eslam.s.m153@gmail.com.
